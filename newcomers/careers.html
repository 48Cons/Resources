Career Opportunities for Spouses

Passport status, on-base employment, local UK work and spouse employment support.

Documents and status

Make sure official passport and UK status documentation are correct before relying on them for residency or employment.

On-base employment

Common pathways include USAJobs, NAF, AAFES, DeCA, DoDEA and private defense contractors.

Off-base employment

Local work eligibility depends on immigration and status documentation. Local income may be subject to UK tax and National Insurance.

Remote work and home businesses

Remote U.S. work can create UK tax and employment-law obligations. Home-based businesses may require approval.

Employment support

The Military & Family Readiness Center can help with resumes, UK-style CVs, interviews and local employment guidance.
