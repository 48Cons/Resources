Temporary Lodging

What to expect while you are between arrival and permanent housing.

Securing lodging

RAF Lakenheath temporary lodging is coordinated through Liberty Lodge. If authorized on-base lodging is unavailable, staff may issue documentation for approved off-base lodging.

Upfront costs

Temporary lodging can require a valid card and significant upfront payment before travel claims or allowances are settled.

Military and civilian allowances

Active-duty personnel generally use TLA rules, while federal civilians use TQSA rules. The processes differ.

Traveling with pets

Pet-friendly rooms are limited. Reserve early and confirm current pet fees, limits and health-record requirements.
