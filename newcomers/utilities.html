Setting Up Utilities

Internet, electricity, water and the small details that turn a new rental into a functioning home.

Internet

Check broadband availability with the exact postcode and review contract length before signing.

Electricity

Photograph the meter reading on move-in day and contact the existing supplier so your account begins from the correct reading.

Water

Water is regional. Around the local area, the source guide identifies Anglian Water as the common supplier.

Other essentials

Ask Housing about the correct council-tax exemption process, understand TV Licence rules and consider contents insurance.
