Mobile Phone Services

Keep access to your U.S. accounts while setting up a practical UK mobile plan.

Why two numbers can help

A dual-SIM or eSIM-capable phone can keep a U.S. number active for banking, verification codes and family while also giving you a UK number for landlords, utilities and local services.

Keeping your U.S. number

Decide before leaving the United States. Some members use Google Fi under its military exception, while others transfer a number to Google Voice. Carrier rules and pricing change, so confirm current eligibility.

UK mobile options

Budget-friendly UK providers in the guide include Giffgaff, SMARTY and VOXI. Larger operators include EE and Vodafone. Coverage can vary by village.

Before your flight

Review two-factor authentication for banks and other critical accounts so losing access to a U.S. SMS number does not lock you out.
