Base Guide

Welcome to the United Kingdom and to the historic Liberty Wing!

Moving to a new military installation is always an adventure, but relocating overseas adds an extra layer of excitement and logistical

puzzles.

To help you hit the ground running, we’ve put together this friendly,

go-to guide to the essential facilities on RAF Lakenheath. Based directly on the official base map, we've broken down every listing

under Resources and Fun so you can easily navigate your new home. Bookmark this page, keep it handy, and let’s get exploring!

Click on BASE DIRECTORY to get direct access to hours of operations, phone numbers and base location information to every

building on base: BASE DIRECTORY

Essential Base Resources Explained

Money & Banking

Managing two currencies (US Dollars and British Pounds) can feel tricky at first, but these on-base spots make it simple:

● ATMs (Multiple Locations): Located conveniently around the

base, these machines dispense both USD ($) and GBP (£) so you always have local cash ready for off-base trips. ● Community Bank (Bldg 989): Your local source for

dual-currency banking. They can assist you with setting up accounts, exchanging currency, and establishing direct debits for UK utility bills. https://www.dodcommunitybank.com/home/locations/united _kingdom/lakenheath [link]

● Keesler Federal Credit Union (Bldg 987): A trusted cooperative

banking option offering US-based savings, checking, loans, and credit services to keep your financial health on track. https://www.kfcu.org/branches-atms

Vehicle & Logistics

If you brought a car or plan on buying one locally, you'll become very familiar with these locations:

● Auto Hobby & Car Wash (Bldg 1108): If you prefer a hands-on

approach to vehicle maintenance, rent a bay and use their professional lifts and tools. If you just need a quick clean after driving through the muddy Suffolk countryside, the car wash is right here. https://lakenheathfss.com/auto-hobby-complex/

Health & Housing

Taking care of your family’s physical well-being and finding a cozy place to live are top priorities:

● Hospital (Bldg 932): Run by the 48th Medical Group, this

facility offers comprehensive medical care, outpatient clinics, emergency services, pediatric care, and pharmacy services to keep you and your family healthy. ● Housing Office (Bldg 429): Your very first stop for lodging

logistics. Whether you are aiming to live in on-base housing or looking to rent a charming English cottage off-base, the housing team will guide you through the contracts and processes. ● Liberty Lodge Front Desk (Bldg 984): The base's temporary

lodging facility. It offers clean, comfortable, and pet-friendly rooms for families transitioning in or out of the base. Administration & Support

From ID cards to tax relief, these offices keep you legally and professionally squared away:

● Base Education & Training (Bldg 948): Want to start a

degree or take professional military exams? This office provides educational counseling, testing services, and hosts representatives from various universities. ● Finance (Bldg 977): Head here to resolve military pay issues,

set up your Overseas Housing Allowance (OHA), and establish your Cost of Living Allowance (COLA). ● MPF / Pass & ID (Bldg 977): The Military Personnel Flight

handles ID cards, DEERS updates, and the vital paperwork required to legally live and drive in the United Kingdom. ● NAF Human Resources (Bldg 977): Looking for employment

opportunities on base? The Non-Appropriated Funds HR office lists jobs for recreation facilities, clubs, lodging, and more.

● VAT Relief Program Office (Bldg 977): One of the best

financial perks of being stationed in the UK! The Value Added Tax (VAT) in the UK is 20%, but eligible service members can use this office to purchase high-value items (like cars, furniture, or electronics) tax-free. ● Hardcopies Print Shop (Bldg 977): Need flyers, official

documents, banners, or high-quality prints? This shop provides professional-grade printing services right on base. ● Liberty Wing Chapel (Bldg 990): Providing spiritual support,

counseling, religious education, and community worship services for a wide variety of faiths and denominations. ● Post Office (Bldg 1025): Your lifeline to home! Head here to set

up your personal APO box so you can receive mail and packages from the US just like you would stateside.

Hobbies & Skills

● Arts & Crafts Center (Bldg 906): From custom framing and

engraving to pottery and crafting classes, this center is the creative heart of the base. ● Wood Shop (Bldg 1107): A fully equipped workspace for

woodworking enthusiasts. Whether you want to build custom furniture or work on a DIY home project, you’ll find the heavy machinery and safety instruction you need here. Travel & Community Hubs

● Information, Tickets, & Travel (ITT) (Bldg 988): Your

gateway to Europe! The ITT office offers heavily discounted tickets to London West End shows, local theme parks, and historic castles. They also organize guided bus tours across

the UK and can help you book cheap flights and hotels for European getaways. ● USO (Bldg 670): A warm, welcoming "home away from home"

run by volunteers. Stop by for free snacks, gaming consoles, comfortable lounge chairs, free Wi-Fi, and regular community events. ● Eagles' Landing - Community Event Center (Bldg 958): A

large, multi-purpose venue that hosts major base events, social gatherings, trivia nights, and special dinners. ● Page Community Center (Bldg 900): A central hub hosting

local community gatherings, family activities, and social programs to help you meet your neighbors. Sports, Recreation, & Entertainment

● Fitness & Sports Center (Bldg 904): Stay in peak physical

condition with state-of-the-art cardio and weight equipment, an indoor running track, basketball courts, group fitness classes, and competitive intramural sports leagues. ● Breckland Pines Golf Course (Bldg 1320): Enjoy a round of

golf on this beautiful 9-hole course located right on base. It's perfect for both seasoned golfers and beginners, and features a great clubhouse for a post-game meal. ● Liberty Lanes Bowling Center (Bldg 657): Perfect for family

fun nights or weekend hangouts, featuring modern lanes, cosmic bowling events, and a snack bar serving up classic diner favorites. ● Outdoor Recreation (Bldg 1109): The UK is beautiful, and

Outdoor Rec is here to help you experience it. Rent camping gear, mountain bikes, kayaks, and bouncy castles, or sign up for organized outdoor adventure trips like hiking, rock climbing, and skiing.

● Reel Time Movie Theater (Bldg 694): Enjoy the latest

Hollywood blockbusters on the big screen without leaving the base. Popcorn, drinks, and snacks are fully stocked!
