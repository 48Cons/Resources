Vehicle Guide

A starting point for importing a U.S. vehicle, buying locally, arranging rentals and driving on the left.

Importing a U.S. vehicle

The source guide organizes the process around VPC drop-off and pickup, registration, plates, road tax, light conversions, IVA or MOT requirements and insurance.

Buying a vehicle in the UK

Plan to understand the registration process, MOT status and the documents needed before buying locally.

Taxis and rentals

A rental can bridge the gap while waiting for an imported vehicle or shopping for a local car. Plan airport-to-lodging transportation in advance.

Driving in the UK

Study local road rules, roundabouts, speed limits and left-side driving before arrival. The first-week guide also covers local licensing.
