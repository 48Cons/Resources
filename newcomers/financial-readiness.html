Financial Readiness

Prepare for exchange rates, banking, deposits and upfront PCS costs.

Foreign transaction fees

Review your cards before moving. Cards that charge foreign transaction fees can become expensive quickly when used for everyday GBP purchases.

Paying in pounds

Off-base rent and many local services use UK bank transfers. Multi-currency services such as Wise can make USD to GBP transfers easier.

Two-factor authentication

Review how your financial accounts send security codes before leaving the U.S. An authenticator app or another non-SMS method can reduce lockout risk.

Transition cushion

Temporary lodging, deposits, rentals and a local vehicle can require substantial cash before allowances or reimbursements arrive.
