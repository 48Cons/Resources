Newcomer guide

Your First Week on Station

A simple roadmap for the briefings, paperwork and practical tasks that tend to fill your first days.

Sponsor check-in

Your sponsor helps with temporary lodging, squadron arrival and registration for required in-processing.

Newcomers orientation

Expect briefings covering in-processing, finance, travel vouchers, medical and TRICARE information, local safety and cultural adaptation.

Driving and local licensing

Study the UK Highway Code before arrival and bring a valid stateside driver's license.

ID and benefits verification

Dependents and DoD civilians should ensure ID cards reflect the logistical support privileges they are entitled to use.

Set up your APO

Bring a copy of your orders to establish an APO box so you can begin receiving mail and online orders.
