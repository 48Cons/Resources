Child Care

The main childcare pathways at RAF Lakenheath and how to start planning before you arrive.

Your options

The source guide outlines Child Development Centers, Family Child Care providers and the Military Child Care in Your Neighborhood subsidy program.

Child Development Centers

Demand can be high. Families can create a profile and request care through MilitaryChildCare.com before the PCS.

Family Child Care

Licensed in-home providers can offer more flexible schedules and nonstandard-shift care.

Off-base subsidy

Contact the Community Child Care Coordinator before beginning care so eligibility and provider approval are in place.
