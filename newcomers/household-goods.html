Household Goods Shipment Prep

What to bring, what may be better left behind and how to bridge the gap while your shipment is in transit.

What to leave behind

Think carefully about large U.S. appliances, single-voltage high-wattage appliances and oversized furniture. British homes can have narrow doors and smaller rooms.

What to bring

Dual-voltage electronics, storage items, weather-ready clothing and a hand-carried moving binder can be especially useful.

While you wait

Plan for APO delivery, local shopping, the BX, Commissary and temporary loan programs while household goods are in transit.

Buy locally when it makes sense

UK furniture and appliances are designed for local voltage and room sizes. Measure your home before buying large pieces.
