Travel Documents & Entry Requirements

What military members, federal civilians and command-sponsored family members should prepare before entering the UK.

Start with the right documents

Military members should carry NATO orders, military ID and a valid tourist passport. Command-sponsored military dependents use the appropriate no-fee passport for official travel. DoD civilian employees and authorized dependents use official passports for government-directed travel.

Military dependents

Command sponsorship is separate from DEERS enrollment. Dependents should be listed on the sponsor's PCS orders and should carry the official passport required for their status plus a tourist passport for leisure travel.

DoD civilians

Civilian employees and authorized dependents should use the official passport and travel orders required for their PCS status. A personal tourist passport is still needed for leisure travel.

Passport office

The source guide lists RAF Lakenheath Passport Office at Commercial 01638 52 1210, DSN 226-1210 and 48fss.passports@us.af.mil.
