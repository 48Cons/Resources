Essential Phone Apps

A curated starter list for parking, food, trains, travel, navigation, shopping and military life.

Parking

RingGo, PayByPhone and JustPark are commonly used for cashless parking.

Food

Just Eat is common in smaller towns. Deliveroo and Uber Eats are stronger in larger towns and cities.

Travel

Trainline is useful for national rail planning. Skyscanner can help compare flights.

Navigation

Waze and Google Maps are useful for driving. Citymapper is particularly helpful in London.

Military and health

USAF Connect, MyCare Overseas and MHS GENESIS are highlighted in the source guide.
