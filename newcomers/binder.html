Welcome to the UK: Your Newcomer’s Guide to Life Across the Pond

Moving from the United States to the United Kingdom is an exciting adventure, but an overseas move comes with plenty of questions, paperwork, and unfamiliar processes. This Newcomer’s Guide brings together the information you’ll need to prepare for your move, get settled after arrival, and confidently navigate everyday life in the UK.
