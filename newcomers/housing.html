Housing

On-base housing, off-base rentals and local communities around RAF Lakenheath.

On-base housing

The Military Housing Office supports the tri-base area. Active-duty members with command-sponsored dependents may be eligible for Military Family Housing.

Off-base rentals

British homes are often smaller than U.S. homes. Narrow stairways, smaller rooms and limited built-in storage can affect what furniture fits.

Local communities

The source guide covers Beck Row, Brandon, Thetford, Bury St Edmunds, Red Lodge, Newmarket, Cambridge and Ely.

Housing contacts

The guide lists Housing Assistance at +44 (0)1638 52 2000 and DSN 314-226-2000.
