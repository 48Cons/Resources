Schools & Education

Understand your choices between DoDEA, British schools and home schooling in the tri-base area.

School Liaison

Start with the School Liaison Program for transitions, credit transfers and local options. The guide lists Building 950 and DSN 226-5077/5078.

DoDEA

Hand-carry orders, identity documents, immunization records, report cards, testing records and any IEP documentation.

British schools

Applications are managed by the county where you live. Local school transport is often the family's responsibility.

Home schooling

Home schooling is legal in the UK, and eligible military dependents may still have access to selected DoDEA activities and support.
