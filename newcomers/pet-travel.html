Pet Travel

A practical overview of the health timeline and common transportation routes for moving dogs and cats to Great Britain.

Health timeline

The guide calls for an ISO-compliant microchip before or on the same day as the rabies vaccination, the required waiting period, an endorsed health certificate in the correct travel window and tapeworm treatment for dogs in the required pre-arrival period.

Manifest cargo

Direct pet travel into the UK generally uses an approved live-animal cargo process and may involve Heathrow Animal Reception Centre and a clearing agent.

Mainland Europe route

Some families with smaller pets fly into mainland Europe on an airline that permits in-cabin pets and then enter Great Britain by an approved ground or ferry route.

Professional pet shippers

Full-service relocation companies can coordinate paperwork, crates, flights, ground transportation and customs clearance.
