RESOURCES

Relocating to RAF Lakenheath is an exciting adventure, but it also comes with a mountain

of unique questions. Whether you are wondering how to navigate housing, where to

find the best Sunday roast, or how to set up your UK phone plan, you do not have to figure

it out alone.

While your unit’s Key Support Liaisons (KSLs) should always be your first point of contact for

official resources, connecting with the local community online is one of the fastest ways to

settle in. The Liberty Wing community is incredibly active online, offering an absolute

goldmine of firsthand PCS tips, housing

recommendations, and local classifieds. To

help you hit the ground running, we have compiled a comprehensive list of the most

useful Facebook groups every incoming

member and family member can join.

Community & Support Hubs

These groups serve as your primary networks for general advice, meeting people, and learning how to navigate everyday life.

● Things I wish I knew Before I PCS’d: This is a group for members

stationed all across the world. Members share tips for PCS seasons. ● Tribase Spouses & AD RAF Lakenheath/Mildenhall/Feltwell: One of

the most active private networks for sharing firsthand relocation experiences and advice. ● Tribase Spouses - Off Base Employment: This group helps spouses of

active-duty members and contractors navigate the UK job market and network while seeking or maintaining off-base employment. It strictly focuses on local UK hiring and does not cover on-base positions, remote US work, or business startups. ● Tribase-A Village Away From Home: Support group for Tribase

members.

Official Base Organizations & Services

For official operational hours, support services, and family programs, follow these institutional pages and groups.

● 48th Force Support Squadron: The hub for on-base events,

Information, Tickets, and Travel (ITT), and dining facilities.

● RAF Lakenheath Official Page: Run by the 48th Fighter Wing for direct

base-wide notifications and mission updates. ● Team Mildenhall Military & Family Readiness Center: Offers

comprehensive transition support, financial readiness resources, and workshops. ● USO United Kingdom: A fantastic organization providing a touch of

home through community events, free snacks, deployment support, and holiday celebrations for service members and their families. ● RAF Lakenheath, Arts & Crafts Center: The go-to page for creative

classes, framing services, custom embroidery, engraving, and picking up DIY supplies for your next home project. ● RAF Lakenheath Airman’s Attic: A volunteer-run resource offering

free household items, uniforms, and baby gear to junior enlisted airmen and their families during their transition. ● Swanky Seconds – Lakenheath Thrift Shop: A popular on-base

consignment and thrift shop where you can score hidden gems, cheap winter gear, and affordable home decor. ● Liberty Library: Great for community programs, book clubs, and free

resources for all ages. ● RAF Lakenheath Housing Management Office: Essential for staying

up to date on base housing policies, waitlists, maintenance alerts, and off-base rental guidelines. ● RAF Lakenheath Airmen’s Food Pantry: A supportive community

initiative providing free groceries and essential pantry staples to airmen and families facing temporary food insecurity or PCS transitions.

Sales, Housing, & Classifieds

Because traditional Facebook Marketplace rules can be tricky for military personnel, these localized groups are crucial for buying and selling PCS goods.

● RAFL/M/F Tri-Base Area Online Yard Sale: A broad classifieds page

for furniture, household items, and uniform pieces. ● Tribase Buy Nothing: Specifically geared toward incoming and

outgoing families looking to offload or acquire household goods for free. ● (RAF Feltwell, Mildenhall, Lakenheath) & surrounding areas

Sales/Wanted Ads: A high-traffic community classifieds page ideal for finding local items for sale, posting wanted ads, or scouting out nearby off-base housing.

Lemon Lot & Vehicles

● The Official Lakenheath / Mildenhall Auto Car Sales: A popular

private hub dedicated to buying and selling cars across the Suffolk and Norfolk areas. ● Official RAF Lakenheath/Mildenhall Lemon Lot Car Sales: A

high-traffic group specifically built for Tri-Base community members, including those at Alconbury and Molesworth. ● Lemon Lot @ RAF Lakenheath/Mildenhall/Alconbury: Excellent for

browsing public automotive listings, tracking down reliable daily drivers, and comparing vehicle options.

Pets & Shipping

● UK Tri-Base Pet Services & Activities: Perfect for finding local pet

sitters, dog walkers, and veterinarian recommendations around Suffolk. ● Military PCS with Pets: Great to find information on how to PCS with

your pets. Navigating the UK's strict pet entry requirements can be daunting; use this group for flight booking tips and vet paperwork checklists.

Kids & Family Life

● Things To Do With The Kids In And Around Lakenheath &

surrounding areas: A community for parents to coordinate playdates, share school experiences, and trade baby gear.

Events & Local Activities

● Tri-Base and Beyond Events: Find out about upcoming British markets,

festivals, and weekend trip ideas across the UK.

KEY SUPPORT LIAISONS

Key Support Liaisons (KSLs) serve as a vital lifeline for military families, bridging the gap between unit leadership and home life. As command-appointed volunteers, they provide trusted peer-to-peer support, welcome new families to the area, and offer a reassuring network of care during deployments. Whether you are navigating a stressful relocation, facing a deployment separation, or just trying to find the right resource on base, your KSL is here to ensure you never have to figure it out alone.
